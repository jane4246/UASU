Who We Are

Dedicated to the Service & Honor of Academia

The Universities Academic Staff Union (UASU) - University of Embu Chapter is the official bargaining body representing academic teaching staff at the University of Embu.

Our Mission

To champion the welfare, rights, and professional status of academic staff while advocating for quality higher education, research support, and academic freedom in Embu.

Our Vision

A highly empowered, motivated, and equitable academic community where research, teaching, and administrative support thrive with absolute integrity and fair compensation.

Welfare Priority

Providing robust medical cover oversight, emergency welfare funds, legal defense assistance, and pension equity for all academic cadres in the university.
