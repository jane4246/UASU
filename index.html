Who We Are

Dedicated to the Service & Honor of Academia

The Universities Academic Staff Union (UASU) - University of Embu Chapter is the official bargaining body representing academic teaching staff at the University of Embu.

Our Mission

To champion the welfare, rights, and professional status of academic staff while advocating for quality higher education, research support, and academic freedom in Embu.

Our Vision

A highly empowered, motivated, and equitable academic community where research, teaching, and administrative support thrive with absolute integrity and fair compensation.

Welfare Priority

Providing robust medical cover oversight, emergency welfare funds, legal defense assistance, and pension equity for all academic cadres in the university.

2025–2029 CBA Negotiations – Current Status

2025–2029 CBA Negotiations – Current Status The 2025–2029 National CBA remains under negotiation between UASU, IPUCCF, the Government and other relevant stakeholders. The proposed agreement covers salaries, allowances, promotions, research support and other terms and conditions of service. UASU has continued to engage stakeholders on the outstanding issues, with financing of the proposed CBA remaining a key matter in the negotiations. Members are advised that the **2025–2029 National CBA has not yet been concluded or implemented**. The Chapter will continue to keep members informed through verified official updates as negotiations progress.

Member FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

All academic teaching staff employed by University of Embu, including Tutorial Fellows, Lecturers, Senior Lecturers, Associate Professors, and Full Professors are eligible.
Union dues are set at 1% of basic salary as mandated by the UASU Constitution. They are deducted automatically via the university payroll check-off system after submitting the signed authorization form.
Members receive legal advocacy during internal proceedings, support on promotion appeals, medical cover oversight, and representation during CBA negotiations.
